# FST-DE — Dark Energy research programme

Version 1.13, published 2026-10-02: [10.5281/zenodo.23093457](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23093457). [Concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19036235).

The current mathematical result is the unique Lambert-W minimizer of a specified strictly convex one-variable function with B,C > 0. Connecting this reduced result to cosmology remains ongoing research. The physical identification of its density, conservation or exchange law, subtraction term, RG coefficients, scalar history, screening profile, horizon/ensemble and official likelihood remain open. The conditional Hubble-scale ansatz is approximately 290 below observation. The Pöschl-Teller hilltop is unstable; standard metric f(R) gives slip 1/2 (or reciprocal 2) and lensing Sigma ~ 1/F. Earlier forecasts of 5/4, 9/8 and a stable attractor remain explicitly discarded.

Version 1.13 improves Figure 1 with separate space for headings, status lines and mathematical content in both languages. The abstract, introduction and status tables describe the current programme directly; a dedicated development note lists the changes since v1.12. The reduced mathematical core, bibliography, numerical values and scientific evidence status are unchanged. This presentation update contributes no new physical evidence or computation.

Version 1.12 established the corrected source identity and bibliographic metadata, distinguished canonical interacting quintessence from phantom-field quintom examples, and documented the physical-identification requirements and AI contribution. The complete publication history remains on Zenodo.
