## English

This is a **major correction of the previously published mathematical claims**. The earlier proof does not establish the advertised spatial-volume-independent physical Wilson transfer gap.

- **Influence-count correction:** a four-dimensional interior link meets six plaquettes, each containing three other links. Up to eighteen neighbor-link incidences, not six, must be counted in the pairwise Dobrushin row sum; the earlier numerical threshold is not supported by that proof.
- **Typical-set correction:** the old restricted Poincaré argument omitted geometric and cross-region-flow hypotheses and contained an invalid concentration scaling. Its claimed large-beta improvement is withdrawn.
- **Dynamics separation:** the spectral gap of the heat-bath/Glauber resampling generator is not identified with the reflection-positive physical time-transfer gap without a separate proven comparison.
- **What remains:** a conservative strong-coupling uniqueness criterion and an explicitly spatial-volume-dependent finite-lattice transfer estimate. Additional Schur and summable-loss lemmas are **abstract and conditional**.
- **What is not proved:** a noncircular uniform infrared anchor, summable relative physical Yang–Mills RG errors, nontrivial OS-continuum construction or a four-dimensional continuum mass gap.
- **Publication format:** a six-page English corrigendum and a six-page German corrigendum replace the previously overclaimed manuscript. Earlier versions remain accessible and permanently citable. The redundant combined file is not included.
- AI-assisted drafting and exploratory mathematics are disclosed; computer checks are not peer review.

- Die Dobrushin-Zeilensumme muss bei der verwendeten Paarabschätzung bis zu 18 Kanteninzidenzen berücksichtigen, nicht nur sechs.
